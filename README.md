## Why this repo exists

Both stores require a publicly reachable privacy policy for family/children's apps, and the
Creative Commons licences used by the app require attribution to be visible to users. Keeping
these pages in a public repository makes the claims checkable: the app ships no network
permission, and the data files it derives from HYG are published here under the same
CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

## Data files

- `data/stars.json` - the derived HYG subset shipped in the app (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- `data/constellations.json` - constellation figures and story text (CC BY 4.0, original to Solar Star)
