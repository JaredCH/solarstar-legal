Solar Star

The solar system and night sky for curious kids · version 1.0.0

This site holds the public legal and support pages for the Solar Star app on the Amazon Appstore and Google Play. Nothing here is required to use the app: Solar Star works entirely offline and collects no data.

At a glance

  • Price: one-time purchase, $5.99. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.
  • Data collected: none. The app has no internet permission.
  • Audience: children aged 5–12, families and classrooms.
  • Platforms: Amazon Fire tablets (Fire OS 7+) and Android 7.0+ phones and tablets.
