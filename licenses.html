Licenses & attribution

Solar Star 1.0.0 · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Solar Star is a paid app, so every third-party asset below was chosen for a licence that allows commercial distribution. The attributions that the licences require also appear inside the app under Settings → About & credits.

CC BY-SA 4.0

Star catalogue

HYG Database v4.0 by David Nash (astronexus) — github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.

Solar Star ships a derived subset: every star brighter than magnitude 6.5 (8,920 stars), reduced to right ascension, declination, magnitude and colour index. Because it is a derivative of the HYG database, that data file is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. The share-alike obligation covers the data file only, not the app's code, artwork or audio.

The derived file is published for download: data/stars.json (human-readable) and data/constellations.json (constellation figures and story text, original to Solar Star, released under CC BY 4.0).
