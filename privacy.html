Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version: Solar Star collects nothing. It cannot connect to the internet, because it does not have permission to.

Who we are

Solar Star is an educational app for children aged 5–12, published by Jared Hicks ("we", "us"). This notice explains what the app does with information. Contact details are at the end.

What we collect

Nothing. Solar Star collects no personal information and no device information. Specifically it does not collect, transmit or store:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers or account details (there are no accounts);
  • location data of any kind;
  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IMEI, MAC address or serial number;
  • usage analytics, crash reports or telemetry;
  • photos, microphone audio, contacts or files.

Internet access

Solar Star is built without the Android INTERNET permission. The operating system therefore prevents the app from opening a network connection even if it tried, and it never tries. There are no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no third-party network libraries in the app.

You can verify this yourself on any Android device with the APK:

aapt2 dump permissions SolarStar.apk
Expected output: only the package name. No permissions at all.

Children's privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K and similar)

Solar Star is designed for children and intended to be used by families, schools and libraries. We comply with children's privacy rules by not collecting anything from anyone:

  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13, or from anyone else.
  • There is no advertising, no behavioural tracking and no profiling.
  • There are no external links in the app. The screen that names our contact channel sits behind a parental gate: a multiplication question a young child cannot answer.
  • Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for a parent to review, correct or delete. If you believe we hold information about your child, contact us and we will confirm that we do not.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a small preferences file in its own private storage on the device. It records only:

  • whether music, sound effects and spoken narration are switched on;
  • whether orbit trails and planet names are shown;
  • whether "playful" or "real" planet sizes was last selected.

That file contains no identifiers, never leaves the device, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Android cloud backup is disabled for Solar Star, so even these preferences are not copied to Google Drive.

Third-party services

None. The app makes no network requests. Narration audio is pre-recorded and shipped inside the app; it does not use your device's speech engine or any online service at runtime.

Purchases

Solar Star is a single paid download. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no advertisements. Payment is handled entirely by the store you bought it from (Amazon Appstore or Google Play). We never see your payment details.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to retain or delete. Uninstalling the app removes the local preferences file described above.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address and the app's store listing will show a new version number. Because the app cannot communicate, any change reaches you as an ordinary app update.

Contact

Email hicksjaredc@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/JaredCH/solarstar-legal. We aim to reply within a few business days.
