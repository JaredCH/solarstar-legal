Contact us

The fastest way to reach a human is to open an issue here: github.com/JaredCH/solarstar-legal/issues. Please include your device model, the store you bought the app from, and what happened.

We aim to reply within a few business days. Refunds are handled by the store you purchased from, not by us.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes, entirely. Solar Star has no internet permission at all. Airplane mode makes no difference to it.

Why is there no sound?

Tap the speaker button at the top right, then check Settings → Sound. Spoken facts and sound effects have separate switches, and a device in silent mode will still mute media on some Fire tablets.

How do I turn off the narration?

Settings → Spoken facts. The text stays on screen and the read-along highlighting turns off with the audio.

The planets are not in true scale. Is that a mistake?

No. Distances are compressed so the whole system fits on screen, which is what makes it explorable for a child. Tap Playful sizes in the time bar to switch to Real sizes and see how the planets compare to each other.

How do I reach the credits or privacy notice in the app?

Settings (the gear, top right) → About & credits or Privacy. Both sit behind a parental gate: answer the multiplication question to continue.

What devices are supported?

Amazon Fire tablets running Fire OS 7 or newer (Fire 7, Fire HD 8, Fire HD 10, Fire Max 11) and Android 7.0 or newer phones and tablets.

Will there be more content?

Yes. Updates are planned to add dwarf planets, deep-sky objects and spacecraft. They arrive as normal app updates and cost nothing extra.

Verifying our privacy claim

You do not have to take our word for it. On any Android device with the APK installed (or via adb), run:

aapt2 dump permissions SolarStar.apk

The output contains only the package name — no permissions, therefore no network access, no location, no camera and no microphone.
